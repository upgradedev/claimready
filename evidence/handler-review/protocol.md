# External handler review, protocol

**No handler has been approached, no review has happened, and no outcome is recorded anywhere in
this repository.** This file and `response-form.md` beside it are the instrument, empty. If a
reviewer is ever found, their answers go on a copy of the form and the copy is committed with their
name or with an agreed identifier. Until then every outcome field stays blank, and a blank field
means nobody has answered, not that the answer was good.

Fixed 2026-09-01, before any reviewer was contacted, so that the questions cannot be chosen after
seeing what a reviewer said.

## What is being reviewed

One sealed handler packet, produced by the page and verified independently by the routes in
`docs/handler-verification.md`. The example packet is `docs/handler-packet.example.json`.

## What is being asked

One question, and the whole point is that it is not about the software:

> Given this packet and nothing else, could you open a claim file from it, and what would you have
> to go back to the customer for?

Everything on the form serves that question. Nothing on the form asks whether the page is good,
whether the idea is clever, or whether the reviewer likes it. Those answers are worth nothing and
inviting them is how a review becomes a testimonial.

## Who counts as a reviewer

Somebody who has handled motor first notice of loss for an insurer or a broker, in a role where an
incomplete notice was their problem rather than somebody else's. Not a developer, not a founder, not
anyone with an interest in this entry placing. If the only person available fails that test, the
review does not happen and this file stays empty.

## How it is run, in five steps

1. Send the packet file and `docs/handler-verification.md`. Send nothing else: no demo link, no
   video, no description, no explanation of what the project is for. A reviewer who has been told
   what to think is not measuring anything.
2. Ask them to read the packet cold and answer the form before any conversation.
3. Do not answer questions about intent while they read. Record the question instead, on the form,
   because a question a handler had to ask is a finding about the packet.
4. Take the form back verbatim. No editing for tone, no summarising, no dropping the parts that do
   not flatter the packet.
5. Commit the completed form as it came back, and write one line in `results` naming who reviewed it
   and when. A review that cannot be attributed is not published.

## What may be claimed afterwards

Only what the form says, in the reviewer's words, with the number of reviewers stated every time.
One handler is one handler. It is not "handlers say", it is not validation, and it is not evidence
about any population of claims. The honest limitation already written into the README, that nobody
has run ClaimReady on a real intake, is not removed by a document review, and a completed form does
not change it.

If the review goes badly it is published in full and in the same words as a review that goes well.
That rule is fixed here, before the review, for the same reason the impact study's protocol was
fixed before its runs and then published a negative result.
